Our story

A local bakery, baking every day.

Kenty's Bakery & Cafe is on Main Street in Winchelsea, in the middle of town on the Princes Highway. We bake fresh bread, pies, savouries and slices on site every day, make espresso coffee and run a fresh salad bar.

What we stand for

Simple things, done properly.

Baked on site

Our bakers start early so the cabinets are full of fresh pies, pasties, loaves and slices by the time we open at 8 am.

Fresh & good value

Honest bakery food at fair prices, from a hot pie at lunchtime to a salad roll made fresh to order.

A welcoming stop

Locals drop in every day, and travellers stop on their way along the highway. There's parking right out front and plenty of tables inside.

Work with us

Join the Kenty's team.

We're always keen to hear from friendly people with barista, retail or food-service experience, for full-time and casual roles. Send your résumé to Kentysbakery@gmail.com or drop it in to the shop.
